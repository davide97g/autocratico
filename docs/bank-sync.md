# Bank sync (research)

**Status:** research only, nothing built. Facts verified on 2026-10-06. Provider offers change quickly (GoCardless closed its free tier to new signups within a year), so re-check the provider section before building.

**Open decision:** import the home-banking export only, or also connect through Enable Banking (which adds a third-party exception to the privacy rule, see [Privacy](#privacy)). Recommendation: start with the export import and the matching logic, then add Enable Banking as a second source feeding the same pipeline.

## What PSD2 gives a private person

- **Free on the bank's side.** A bank cannot charge or require a contract for giving account data to an authorised provider. The provider in between can charge.
- **No direct access.** A bank's PSD2 interface (XS2A) only answers licensed providers (AISP) holding an eIDAS certificate. A private person has to go through such a provider.
- **Scope: online payment accounts only.** Current accounts, sometimes cards. Not covered: loans and mortgages, securities accounts, brokers, crypto, often deposit accounts. Some banks don't expose credit cards: Enable Banking's Italy page names UniCredit, Banca Mediolanum and Crédit Agricole.
- **Consent lasts at most 180 days**, then strong authentication again in the bank's app (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/2360, amending the PSD2 RTS; it was 90 days before).
- **One consent at a time.** Many Italian banks keep one active consent per provider and user: a new consent revokes the previous one.
- **Unattended reads: at most 4 per account per 24 hours** (RTS Art. 36(5)(b)). Reads made while the user is actively asking don't count. A sync every 6 hours fits; hourly doesn't.
- **Italian banks** often expose PSD2 through the CBI Globe gateway, others through their own implementation; quality varies. Authentication is a redirect to the bank's app, and automatic app-switch is not consistent.

## Regulatory outlook

- **PSD3 / PSR:** provisional agreement on 27 November 2025, final compromise texts published on 23 April 2026, rules applying around 2028. Adds a dashboard for managing the permissions given. Nothing to wait for.
- **FiDA (open finance: investments, pensions, insurance):** stalled. As of September 2026 no trilogue was scheduled. Brokers stay on screenshots and exports (`docs/finance.md`, Investments).

### Enable Banking

- Registered AISP, supervised by the Finnish FIN-FSA.
- **Restricted mode (free):** register a production application in the Control Panel, choose "Activate by linking accounts", log in at the bank and approve; repeat for each bank. The API then returns only the accounts linked this way. Personal, non-commercial use only; their terms grant no right to business use or to other people's accounts.
- **Auth:** application ID plus a private key (generated by the browser at registration, saved as a `.pem`). Every request carries a JWT signed RS256 (`iss: "enablebanking.com"`, `aud: "api.enablebanking.com"`, expiry up to 1 hour). `node:crypto` is enough; no dependency needed.
- **Flow:** `POST /auth` (bank, `valid_until`) → redirect to the bank for authentication → callback with `code` → `POST /sessions` → account ids → `GET /accounts/{uid}/balances`, `GET /accounts/{uid}/transactions`.
- **Italy coverage** (their Italy page): Intesa Sanpaolo, UniCredit, Banco BPM, BCC Iccrea, Crédit Agricole, BPER, MPS, Mediolanum, BBVA, ING, Poste Italiane. Fineco, Revolut, N26, Hype and Satispay are not on that page; check the full list in the Control Panel.
- **Reference implementations:** Actual Budget (bank sync) and the Firefly III data importer both support it.
- **Check on the first connection:** maximum consent validity for the bank, how much history the first sync returns, whether card accounts appear, transaction pagination.

## Privacy

AGENTS.md keeps personal data on the user's machines, with two deliberate exceptions: Cloudflare and Telegram. With Enable Banking the bank's transactions would pass through a provider's servers in Finland. That would be a third deliberate exception, to be written into AGENTS.md (Rules) and into this doc before building.

The option with no third party: export the movements from home banking (Excel/CSV, sometimes PDF statements) and upload them to the inbox. It gives the same features, but by hand.

## What autocratico would do with transactions

1. **Paid deadlines:** an outgoing transaction whose amount, date and description match an occurrence (F24, car tax, the creditor of a SEPA direct debit) suggests marking it done and records the real amount in `amounts`. Always confirmed by the user, never automatic.
2. **Missing recurring debits:** a SEPA direct debit (SDD/RID) with no matching deadline becomes a suggested new deadline.
3. **Finance app:** expenses are entered by hand today. Transactions would become drafts to categorise and confirm, de-duplicated against those already recorded (`data/finance/links.json`).
4. **Balance:** a Telegram alert when the balance doesn't cover the next payment, with amounts redacted.
5. **Phishing check:** an email about a fine or refund with no matching transaction is a warning sign.
6. **Chat:** questions like "did I pay the car tax?" answered from the transactions.

## Proposed design

- **Shape and matching:** a `BankTransaction` in `packages/core/src/schema.ts` (id, source, date, signed amount, currency, description, counterparty, SDD creditor/mandate when present) and a pure matching function in `packages/core/src/bank.ts`, with tests.
- **Source A, export import:** parse the bank's Excel/CSV export when it reaches the inbox. XLSX needs a parser dependency in `apps/server`; `scripts/` stays standard library only.
- **Source B, Enable Banking:** `apps/server/src/bank.ts`. Application ID and private key in `data/secrets/` (0600), never sent to the browser, same pattern as `gmail.ts` and `finance.ts`. Settings → Bank: connect, show when the consent expires, Telegram reminder 7 days before day 180.
- **Job `bank`:** every 6 hours (at most 4 unattended reads a day). Data in `data/bank/`, server-owned and kept out of the register's git history, like `finance/`.
- **UI:** a "transactions to match" list. Marking an occurrence done or writing to the finance app only happens on confirmation, as `finance.ts` does today.
- **Security:** when built, add `bank.ts` to the security-sensitive list in AGENTS.md.
